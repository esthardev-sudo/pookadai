# 🌸 Pookadai – Fresh Flowers Near You

Pookadai is a simple and user-friendly flower shopping website designed to help users discover fresh flowers from nearby flower shops and place orders easily.

The platform allows users to explore different types of flowers, view flower details and prices, add flowers to their cart, choose delivery options, and place orders.

## 🌸 Project Overview

Finding fresh flowers from nearby shops can sometimes be difficult, especially when users don't know which shop has the flowers they need.

Pookadai aims to provide a convenient platform where users can:

- 🌸 Explore fresh flowers
- 🔍 Search for their favourite flowers
- 📍 Find nearby flower shops
- 🏪 View available flowers in each shop
- 💰 Check flower prices
- 🛒 Add flowers to cart
- 📦 Place orders
- 🚚 Choose door delivery
- 💵 Choose Cash on Delivery
- 💳 Select online payment
- 🌿 Discover daily fresh flowers

## ✨ Key Features

### 🌸 Fresh Flowers
Displays flowers that are freshly available from nearby shops.

### 🔍 Flower Search
Users can search for flowers by name.

### 📍 Nearby Shops
Shows flower shops available near the user's location.

### 🛒 Shopping Cart
Users can add flowers to their cart and manage quantities.

### 📦 Order System
Users can provide their details and place an order.

### 🚚 Door Delivery
Users can choose delivery to their preferred address.

### 💵 Cash on Delivery
Users can select Cash on Delivery as a payment option.

### 💳 Online Payment
The project is designed to support online payment integration.
